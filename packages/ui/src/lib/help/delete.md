# Deleting Files

Deleting files in NexusCommander is quick and easy.

## How to Delete
1. **Select** the files or folders you wish to remove.
2. Press **F8** or **Delete**.
3. A confirmation dialog will appear. Press Enter or click OK to proceed.

## Permanent Deletion vs Trash
By default, NexusCommander attempts to move files to your system's **Trash** or **Recycle Bin**. 
- If you wish to permanently delete files bypassing the trash, hold down **Shift** while pressing **Delete** (e.g., `Shift+Del` or `Shift+F8`).
- **Warning**: Permanent deletion cannot be undone.

## Handling Errors
If a file is locked by another process or you lack the necessary permissions, a dialog will prompt you to skip the file, retry, or abort the entire deletion task.
